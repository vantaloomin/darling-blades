    How the math works

    Every effect on a card is worth some number of points. One point is roughly what one mana buys. Add them up and you get the card's Power.

    The Budget is what the card's cost and rarity should pay for. A higher cost buys more, each colored pip after the first buys a little extra, and rarer cards get a small bonus.

    The Difference is Power minus Budget. Within 0.75 either way, the card is Accurate Value. Beyond that it does more or less than its cost should buy, and past 1.5 it is well outside what the game prints.

    Effects that sit outside a card's colors cost extra. The breakdown lists that as an off-color premium.

    A rate tagged Estimate hasn't been measured in play yet, so treat it as a best guess.

    The game's own cards are costed with this formula. It is a guide: real cards also get playtested before they ship.
