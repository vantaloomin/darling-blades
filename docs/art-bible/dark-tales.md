foam, glass, satin, thorn halos, gilded cages, candlelit libraries, and
midnight-blue magic. Every subject is an adult-coded woman. Preserve the
original parody names and silhouettes; every book, mirror, invitation, page,
and banner stays blank of lettering. Companion entries are hand-authored from
the ratified table and use the house-wide single-figure, middle-band, practical-
prop, directional-effect, and no-text prompt guards.
