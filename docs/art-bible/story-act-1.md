# Story Mode Act 1 art

The 42 still images for Story Mode Act 1 (*The Fallen Mandate*): 18 scene backgrounds and 24 dialogue portraits (12 speakers, 2 expressions each). Owner rulings, 2026-10-12: V1 stills and dialogue inserts for the Act 1 launch, with full animation at the Full Story launch; V2 A new waist-up cutouts drawn from each character's card art; V3 two expressions per speaker; backgrounds at 2560×1440. The owner approved the final set on 2026-10-12. See [plan-story-mode.md](../plan-story-mode.md) for where they are used.

## Files

| Kind | Full size (`public/assets/art/story/`) | Half size (`public/assets/art/story-half/`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Backgrounds `bg-<place>` | 2560×1440 webp, opaque | 1280×720 webp |
| Portraits `pt-<speaker>-<expression>` | 1024×1536 webp with alpha | 512×768 webp with alpha |

The half-size files were made on the art PC alongside the full ones and are committed (unlike `cards-half/`, which `gen-art-halfres` derives). They ship as loose files for now; packing them like card art (`scripts/pack-art.ts`) belongs with the itch.io work, which has to pack every loose art file anyway.

## Rules these images were made to

These carry into any regeneration. They add to the global rules of [index.md](index.md); the scene backdrops of [scene-art.md](../scene-art.md) are a different family.

- **Backgrounds have no people.** No figures, silhouettes or faces, however small: portraits sit on top. A tiny figure on a rock in `bg-changban-bridge` was painted out after review.
- **The left third of every background stays quiet,** low in contrast and detail, so a portrait and the text box can sit over it.
- **Text only where the script names a sign:** TOLL (`bg-meng-ford`), FORTUNES, HONEST ONES (`bg-diviners-tree`) and STAY BACK (`bg-changban-bridge`), in English. Nothing else is lettered.
- **Portraits are drawn from the card art,** waist-up on a flat #c8c8c8 backdrop, then cut out. Every character is unmistakably adult. The officer, the Wei rider and the merchant have no card and are new figures; the officer wears Wei black in Sima Yi's colours.
- **The whole figure stays inside the frame.** Hair, sleeves and weapons end inside the top, left and right edges, with at least 24 px of clear alpha; only the bottom edge, at the waist, is cut, with a soft fade.
- **The second expression is made from the first,** same figure, outfit, crop and scale, so the two match when they swap mid-scene.
- **Exactly two arms and two hands;** tails grow from the tailbone; armour is not mirrored between expressions.
- **Weapons are composited, never redrawn.** Each weapon has one master image (Guan Yu's guandao, Gongsun Zan's two-headed spear, Shanjun's spear, the officer's sword, Penthesilea's axe), drawn rigid with the blade in line with the shaft. In the final portraits the drawn weapon was erased and the master pasted in with rotation, uniform scale and a horizontal flip only, and the original gripping fingers laid back over the shaft. A script on the art PC (`tools\verify_weapons.py`) renders each master through its saved transform and fails any placement that differs. Regenerating with the master as a reference drifts every time; compositing is the only way to keep them exact. The masters and transforms live in the art PC's `DarlingBlades-art\story-act-1\` folder (`refs\`, `logs\weapon-transforms.json`).
- **A regeneration changes the prompt** to match the review note, and the new prompt replaces the one below.

## Prompts

Each prompt is the one last sent to the generator for the kept image, copied unchanged. The generator worked at 1536×1024 (backgrounds) and 1024×1536 (portraits); backgrounds were then upscaled to 2560×1440 and checked at 100%. The weapons in the armed portraits come from the composited masters, not from these prompts. The portrait prompts that say "the first attached image" were run with the first expression as the reference, as the rules above describe.
