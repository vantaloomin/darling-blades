# The Starborne Art Bible

Entries for the locked 151-card shipping set. The **Starborne register** in
[index.md](index.md) section 4b governs every palette, value floor, lighting
pair, species tell, mark depiction, and mechanic-family composition below, and
the global style contract in section 2 remains in force above it.

**Card facts are transcribed from the cut list in
[starborne-overplan.md](../expansions/drafts/starborne-overplan.md)**, which is
the locked design. `src/data/cards/starborne.ts` does not exist yet; when it
lands, `check-art-bible` becomes the authority and every fact line here must
agree with it.

Three register rules bind hardest and are repeated here because they are the
ones a generator breaks first:

- **Biolume cyan `#5ff0e0` is reserved for marks and living light.** Never
  engines, consoles, weapon glow, sky, or ambience.
- **Open starfield is never the whole background.** Every frame is an interior,
  a hull surface, a garden, a cloudbank, or a planetary surface.
- **No readouts.** A console may glow; it may not display anything.

## Wave 1: Ultra Rare (calibration batch)

so they expose a weak register before a hundred commons are generated against
