# Darling Blades 2.0

2.0 adds Core Set II, a 250-card set that goes back to the base set's Three Kingdoms, Greek and Beastkin casts. It also adds Story Mode, a phone layout, and starting life of 25 instead of 20. Every new card was costed at the new life total.

## In this release

- **New set:** Core Set II, 250 cards, built around **the Mandate** and **Sworn**, with three theme decks
- **Story Mode:** Act 1 of *The Fallen Mandate* for three heroines, then an endless run
- **Starting life is 25** in every duel, and the tower was retuned for it
- **Phones:** the game has its own phone layout, in landscape, in the browser
- **New menus on desktop,** built from the same layouts as the phone
- **Duel any Deck:** paste a deck code and play against it, up to a new Master AI
- **A new sound style** you can switch on in Settings
- A balance change, fixes, and a safer save

## New set: Core Set II

In the Three Kingdoms, the Mandate of Heaven is the right to rule. Heaven grants it and takes it back from rulers who fail. In Core Set II it has fallen to the ground. It is now something you can carry onto a battlefield, and anyone who hits its keeper can take it. Wei, Wu, Shu and Jin want it back. Olympus says it is theirs to give. The Beastkin will raid whoever holds it.

The set has 250 cards: 122 commons, 74 rares, 23 SR, 19 SSR and 12 UR. 38 of them are legendary, all of them new characters. All 250 have finished art. The set has its own booster and three theme decks.

### The Mandate

> The Mandate begins unclaimed. At your Dawn, if you hold it, draw a card. When your creatures deal combat damage to the player who holds it, you claim it.

There is one Mandate in each duel. Nobody holds it until a card says "claim the Mandate", so the first claim always comes from a card. After that, any attack that gets damage through to the holder takes it. Damage from spells or from a Hunt does not. It sits as a seal beside whoever holds it, and the duel history records each claim.

42 Core Set II cards claim the Mandate or care who holds it.

### Sworn

> Sworn is active while you control a legendary creature.

Sworn is a condition, not a keyword. A Sworn line only works while you control a legend. In Darlings your Darling counts once she is on the battlefield, so casting her turns your Sworn cards on, and losing her turns them off. 24 cards have a Sworn line. The commons and rares among them work without it.

### Some of the legends

- **Yutu, Jade Rabbit of the Moon Palace**, {4}{W}{U}, 4/4. Skyborne. `Other Beastkin you control get +1/+1. Whenever another Beastkin arrives under your control, draw a card. This happens once each turn.` She is also one of the three Story Mode heroines.
- **Cerberus, Hound of the Three Gates**, {3}{B}{R}{G}, 7/6. Dreaded, Overrun, Deathblade. `Whenever you claim the Mandate, deal 2 damage to each opponent.`
- **Nüwa, Who Mended the Sky**, {5}{W}{U}{B}{R}{G}, 4/4. Tithe. She claims the Mandate when she arrives. For each mana Tithe saves on her cost she arrives with a mark, up to 5. Each mark pays for one of five stones, one per colour: 3 damage, a mark on each of your other creatures, 5 life, two cards, or a Sever.

### Three new theme decks

Each is 500 gold in the shop, one for each of the set's three casts.

- **The Wei Court** (white-black, Three Kingdoms). Wei and Jin officials claim the Mandate as they arrive, and cheap removal stops anyone from taking it back. Lady Bian, the Dragon Banner and the Edict of Rightful Rule pay you for holding it.
- **Jade River Pack** (blue-green, Beastkin). Otters and seals draw cards and send blockers back to hand. Jiuwei and Mulu make every Beastkin bigger, and the Moon Pestle keeps them untouchable.
- **Sworn of Olympus** (red-white, Greek). Cheap Amazons with Sworn, and six legends to turn it on, from Antiope to Hippolyta, Hebe and Hephaestus.

## Story Mode: The Fallen Mandate

Story Mode is a run of duels with a story told between them. Pick a heroine, take one of three opening boons, and climb a map of nine floors to the Act 1 boss. You choose your path through fights, elites, events, shops and rests. Each fight offers a card for your run deck. Shops sell more cards for the run's gold. Story duels are played in the Darlings format, with your heroine as your Darling.

- **Guan Yu, Saint of War** wants to carry the Mandate home to the rightful house, as she swore at the Peach Garden.
- **Persephone, Queen of Two Courts** wants to close the gates to the underworld that its fall cracked open.
- **Yutu, Jade Rabbit of the Moon Palace** wants to steal it for the Moon Palace.

Guan Yu is open from the start. Win a run with her, or play three runs of at least three fights each, and Persephone opens. Persephone opens Yutu the same way.

Act 1 is about six duels, 45 minutes to an hour. Its three elites (Gongsun Zan, Penthesilea and Shanjun) appear only in Story Mode. The boss is Sima Yi, the Patient Serpent, with a stronger deck and a harder AI than she plays in the tower. Each starter deck mixes the base set with Core Set II. When you clear the act you keep one card from your run deck for your collection. Every other card stays with the run.

**Endless.** A heroine who clears Act 1 can go straight on into an endless run. After that you can also start Endless from the Story menu. Each loop is a shorter map that ends at a new claimant to the Mandate. The claimants get harder as the loops go on. From loop 7 they start the duel holding the Mandate, and from loop 8 with extra life. A daily seed gives everyone the same maps for the day, with as many tries as you like. Your best loop is kept, and there are new achievements for clearing loops 3, 5 and 10.

## Starting life is 25

Every duel now starts at 25 life instead of 20. Games run a little longer and there is more time to come back from a bad start. Every Core Set II card was costed at 25.

The tower was measured again at 25, and two changes came out of it:

- **The top floors are harder.** The hardest tier of the tower now plays at the new Expert level.
- **Carmilla (rung 15)** gets basic lands in place of her four tapped Burning Luoyang, so she stays ahead of the rung below her.

Story Mode and the endless run were tuned at 25 as well.

## Phones

The phone version has been rebuilt. On a touch phone held sideways, the game now switches to a layout made for the small screen. Every screen has a phone version: the menus, the Shop, the Collection, the Deck Builder, Draft, Pack Opening, Story Mode and the Duel.

- In the phone Duel, a column beside the board holds the stack, combat, and every choice the game asks you to make.
- There is a full-screen button, and you can add the game to your home screen.
- Held upright, the game asks you to turn the phone.
- To get the desktop layout on a phone, add `?layout=desktop` to the address.

The phone version runs in the browser. There is no app store version.

## Desktop menus

The desktop menus now use the same layouts as the phone, sized for a mouse and a big screen. The main button on each screen is gold. Settings lists its tabs on the left. The Shop shows three decks to a row. The Achievements screen shows the ones you are closest to. The desktop Duel keeps its own layout.

## Duel any Deck

A new tile on the Play screen. Paste a deck code, or pick one of your own decks, and the AI plays it against your active deck. You don't need to own the cards in the code. Choose the opponent's level:

- **Hard**, the same Hard as the tower.
- **Expert** thinks further ahead than Hard.
- **Master** thinks further ahead than Expert.

In measured AI-against-AI games, Expert beats Hard and Master beats Expert in a little under 60% of games. They see only what you see, like every other level. These duels pay no gold.

## Sound

Settings, Audio has a new **Sound style** choice. **Classic** is the game's sound as it has always been, generated by the game itself. **New** uses recorded sound effects and a music loop for each mood, from free CC0 packs by Kenney and Not Jam, and adds card shuffle, draw and pack-tearing sounds, which Classic does not have. Classic stays the default.

## Smaller things

- **The turn 1 banner says who goes first:** "Turn 1 · You go first".
- **Main menu:** a button in the top bar to join the Darling Blades Discord.
- **Settings, play stats** reads "Blocked by browser" when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, which already stopped the game from sending them.
- **Deck Builder:** rename a deck by tapping its title. Your Decks is one scrolling list.
- **Text no longer clips** at the bottom of letters that hang below the line, like g and y.
- **Leaving a Tower or Draft duel before it ends** counts as a loss, as if you had conceded. This closes a way to replay a losing deal once you have seen the opponent's cards.

## Your save

- **A save that can't be read is no longer replaced silently.** The game keeps a copy of it and tells you when the game starts.
- **An older copy of the game can't wipe a newer save.** If an old tab or an old install finds a save from a newer version, it stops with "Newer save found" instead of starting over.
- **If your browser refuses to store your progress,** the game tells you once and points you to Export save on the Profile screen.
- **The game asks your browser to keep its storage,** so the browser is less likely to clear your save to free space.
- **Fast double taps** can no longer buy a pack twice, add a Premium Draft pool twice, or skip a Story card reward.
- **Coming back to the game with the Back button** reloads it, so a tab that sat in the background can't overwrite what another tab saved.

Saves move to a new version with 2.0. A 2.0 save can't be opened by 1.9.
