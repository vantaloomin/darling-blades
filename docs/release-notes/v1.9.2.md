# Patch 1.9.2

A small patch for 1.9. The game now respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control from the first launch. The Collection can sort by set. The Glossary shows that it scrolls. And the Forge, the card designer on the website, got a full polish pass so it looks and feels like part of Darling Blades.

## In this patch

- **Privacy:** a browser with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control on now starts with anonymous play stats switched off
- **Collection:** sort by set, and flip any sort's direction
- **Glossary:** the list shows that it scrolls
- **The Forge:** a full polish pass, from type and buttons to the score panel and phone layout

---

## Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

The game has always sent nothing at all from a browser with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control turned on. But on a first launch, the "Share anonymous play stats" switch still showed On, which looked like you had been opted in. Now, if your browser sends either signal when you first open the game, the switch starts Off. If you already chose a setting after seeing the notice, your choice stays as it is, and you can change it at any time in Settings.

## Collection: sort by set

Sort by now offers Set beside Card rarity, Variant rarity and Name. Sets are listed in release order, newest first, with the rarest cards first inside each set. A new button beside "Owned only" flips the direction of any sort: high or low first, A to Z or Z to A, newest or oldest first.

## Glossary scrolls visibly

The Glossary's scroll bar used to hide behind its rows, so the list looked like it ended. The scroll bar now has its own lane beside the rows, and a soft fade shows when there are more entries above or below.

## The Forge

The Forge got the same three-reviewer pass the game's screens had in 1.9.1, and every finding is fixed:

- **It looks like the game:** buttons, colours and type now come from Darling Blades itself, with one primary action and text no smaller than 11 pixels
- **A clearer score:** the score panel shows the whole sum, with Power, Budget and the difference between them, and its warnings sort into "The game won't allow this card" and "Notes on this score"
- **Hints that help:** cost and stat hints suggest the change that brings the score closest to even, and you can undo them
- **Easier editing:** an action bar that stays in reach, a Your Set button, one-row cost editors, Color Identity as a switch, and the Forge's own confirm box instead of the browser's
- **Touch and phones:** larger touch targets, stronger contrast, a score strip on phones, and the card fits short windows
- **Screen readers:** editor panels, sliders and the score gauge now describe themselves
