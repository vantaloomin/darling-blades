Four fixes from player reports on 1.6.3. Three of them you asked for directly.

**Your mana curve stays on screen while you build.** The repair banner used to
draw straight over the stats block, so any deck that was momentarily illegal,
which is most of the time you spend building one, hid the curve and the colour
balance behind a warning. The summary now lifts clear and blocking issues read
as status lines instead. A saved deck that has gone illegal swaps its dead Save
button for "Fix Deck", opening a repair modal with every issue listed and the
bulk land strip. A deck you are still assembling is simply incomplete, so it
keeps a quiet disabled Save and no alarm.

**The graveyard reads in order again.** It was collapsing duplicates and sorting
by type and cost, so a cycled card landed somewhere in the middle of the grid
and "top" meant nothing on screen. The graveyard is a pile now: one tile per
card, newest first, the top of the pile marked "Top". Card text says so too,
naming the top creature card of your graveyard rather than a vague "another".
Your deck and the Severed zone still collapse, because deck order is hidden
information and should stay that way.

**Preserve finally has a button.** It has been castable since it shipped, but
there was no way to reach it at the moment it mattered.

**The Warchest stops eating your land drop.** Lands live in a pile you have to
open, so nothing on the board nagged you the way a land in hand used to. The
Reserves pile now pulses while a drop is still legal, and ending your turn on an
unused one asks for a second press. You can turn the confirmation off in
Settings, under "Your turn".

Also in this release: 20 new icons, and the Limited deck builder now shows the
curve a draft deck is actually built by.
