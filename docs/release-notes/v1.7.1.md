A patch for 1.7 built from the first day of play: three cards that shipped
weaker or stranger than they read, one engine fix, one new rule for Hauntlink,
and a shop that stops charging you for lists you already own.

**Hauntlink reacts when it should.** A Hauntlink permanent now gets its own
window to move to a new host whenever a trigger is about to resolve and at the
combat damage step, before damage is dealt. Ordinary Charms keep their timing;
only Hauntlink breaks the rule, and only when you can actually pay to move it.
Alongside it, a creature burned to death by a targeted arrival trigger now
dies on the spot instead of surviving to the cleanup step, a bug the same
report uncovered.

**Ramp had a floor missing.** A one-mana card that granted an extra land drop
could put six lands on the table on turn two. Extra land drops are priced
higher now and no card below two mana grants one; eight cards moved to match.

**Three cards, three fixes, all from play.** Chrome Medallion was an Artifact
whose only text was a one-time Foresee; it now Foresees every Dawn, and
Redline Salvage, which had the same shape, became the Ritual it always was. A
rule now guards the whole pool: a permanent must keep doing something after
it lands. Renenutet, Who Measures the Flood, granted two extra land drops at
nine mana against a ten-land reserve, where the second land could never
exist; her Empower now returns a creature card from your graveyard to your
hand and gains you three life. Jade-Crown Elder shipped as a vanilla 6/4
because her authored ability was lost on the way to the card; she is now the
green Yokai lord, and her other Yokai get +1/+0 and Overrun.

**Own the cards, own the deck.** If your collection already holds every card
in a shop deck, that deck is yours: the shop offers Clone Deck instead of Buy,
the first press adds the deck to your library for free, and later presses make
copies. Umbral Antenna also gives up its flavor text so its three abilities
have room to breathe.
