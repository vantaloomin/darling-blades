A patch for 1.8, built from the release review and the first days of play.
Two crashes and two freezes are gone. Removal stops losing half its text.
Opponents use their Duties before combat. There are 21 new achievements, a
long list of screens now sits inside the frame, and there is a new place to
build cards of your own.

**The Forge: build your own cards.** At
[bladedarlings.com/forge/](https://bladedarlings.com/forge/) you can design a
card from scratch or start from any card in the game. The Forge prices it
with the same scorer we use to balance the game: its Power against its
Budget, and whether it comes out Under Value, Accurate Value or Over Value. It
speaks the game's own words, from Attack and Defense to Foresee and Sever.
Save cards into a set and export it, share a card as a link, save it as an
image, and frame your own picture in the art window. Your pictures stay on
your device. A shared link opens the card with game art instead; an exported
set keeps them.

**Two crashes and two freezes are fixed.**

- Reaper's Due, Verdict Under Resin, Black Water, Night-Market Price and
  White-Veil Collapse could crash the duel when they destroyed Barrow-Jarl of
  the Deep Howe or Sitra, Ferrywoman of Two Rivers, and the creature brought
  back chose a target or Foresaw.
- A Tithe or Rite spell could stay on the stack for good when its sacrifice
  brought back a creature that chose a target.
- Three copies of Sitra could bring each other back forever. A death trigger
  that returns a creature now skips a copy of a legend you already control,
  and returns the next creature card instead.

**Removal keeps its second half.** When a Hauntlink was ready to move, seven
removal spells lost everything printed after the kill. They now finish.

A creature's death also resolves at the same moment whether or not a
Hauntlink is ready, including before the next spell on the stack. White-Veil
Collapse and similar spells gain you their life before the game checks
whether anyone has lost.

**Two timing rules changed.**

- **Rite and Tithe.** Sometimes the creature you sacrifice brings back a
  creature that makes a choice. You now make that choice first, then your
  opponent can respond, then the spell resolves. In 1.8, if your opponent held
  no Charm, the spell resolved before the choice.
- **Attacks that Foresee.** Wild Hunt Matriarch, Morrigan and Aine attack and
  then Foresee. A defender holding a Charm now gets to respond to the attack
  after the Foresee. 1.8 skipped that chance.

**The graveyard card you pick is the card you get.** Retell, Whispers,
Preserve, and effects that return a creature from your graveyard now return
the card you chose, even if the graveyard changed before they resolved. In
1.8 they could return the card beside it.

**Opponents use their Duties before combat.** On Medium and Hard, an opponent
now pays for a Duty before attacking when it opens up the attack: tapping a
blocker, removing one, or pumping an attacker. A Duty that wins the game on
the spot is never missed.

**Hauntlink Apex is playable.** It cost {7}{U} and drew a card at each Dawn.
It now costs {3}{U} and Foresees 1 at each Dawn. Linking it still costs
{3}{U}.

**21 new achievements.** Starborne and Drowned Deep get eight each, and Sands
of the Duat gets five more to reach eight. That makes 108 in all, worth 13,250
more gold. If you have already done what one asks, it unlocks the next time
you open the game. Every achievement now shows its whole description (26 were
cut off), and progress only reads 100% once it is done.

**In a duel.**

- Undo turns off after anything shows you a hidden card, such as a draw or a
  Foresee, and says why.
- A permanent with a Duty you can use wears a gold Duty tag, and the history
  says what your opponent's Duty did.
- Concede asks you to tap or click again, and stands down after four seconds.
- A creature with Rage is always in your attack. The button reads Attack with
  the count, and never offers to skip combat.
- A move the game refuses leaves one plain line in the history instead of an
  error message.
- The piles, both life totals and the Darling tax sit inside the frame.
- A creature returned to its owner's hand no longer shows as "died" in the
  history, and no longer counts toward the daily quests about creatures dying.

**Building decks.**

- Save Deck always works. An unfinished deck saves as it is, and reads "Not
  playable yet" everywhere until it is legal.
- Leaving with unsaved changes asks first: Save Deck, Discard Changes or Keep
  Editing. The Decks menu, the format switch and Warchest edits no longer save
  your deck without asking.
- Deleting a deck says Tap or Click to match how you play, and reminds you the
  cards stay in your collection.

**Drafting and menus.**

- After a Premium draft, the note says how many cards joined your collection,
  and how many were duplicates that turned into gold.
- The Premium draft inspector counts only plain copies toward what turns into
  gold.
- The Limited deck builder shows mana pips instead of letters.
- Both Limited screens ask the same "Leave draft?" question.
- Screen titles sit on one line inside the frame. The Profile screen was
  rebuilt, and nothing on it overlaps anymore.
- Every card that prints Mark, Propagate, Quest or Sever explains it in its
  help.

**Art.**

- Seventeen card arts were redrawn, most with more room above the head.
- Art that arrives after a card is already on screen now appears in place.
  Before, the card kept its placeholder until you reloaded.
- Phones on bladedarlings.com load half-size card art, which is much quicker.

**Play stats.** The card summary now goes each time you leave or close the
game, and each card is counted once per session. Before, only the first one
went, so anything played after you switched away was never counted. The
privacy policy says so, and now names Cloudflare, which serves
bladedarlings.com. Nothing new is collected, and the switch in Settings works
as before.

**Replays** saved on 1.8 can't be played back on 1.8.1.
