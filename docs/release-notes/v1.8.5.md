A balance patch. It started with a card a player built in the Forge: Lu Bu
as a 10/1 with Twin Blades, Skyborne and Untouchable for three mana, which
the Forge called Under Value. The math behind every card priced a keyword
the same on any creature, so Twin Blades cost as much on a 1/1 as on a 10/1.
1.8.5 rebuilds that math, prices every card with it again, and changes 90
cards.

**Keywords now scale with Attack.** Twin Blades, Skyborne, First Blade, Blood
Oath and Warcry are worth more on a creature that hits harder. Bulwark costs
more on a creature that could have hit hard, and Deathblade matters most on a
small one. Each rate was measured by playing hundreds of thousands of games
between the game's own decks, and checked against Magic's card pool through
2020.

**90 cards change: 27 are toned down and 63 are improved.** The full list is
below.

- A card only got weaker if it wins games. Every proposed nerf was checked
  against our deck-building simulation, which keeps the cards that win. It
  picks 27 of them, including Lu Bu, Storm Surge, Reaper's Due and The Price,
  and those are the cards toned down. It never picked the other 48, so those
  cards stay exactly as they were.
- Most improvements go to cards the new math shows are too weak for their
  cost.

**Ramp is priced by when you cast it.** You have ten lands in your reserve and
play one a turn, so an extra land drop late in the game only gets you to ten a
little sooner. The math used to price it the same at any cost. Six ramp cards
are stronger now:

- Flood-Measure Vessel costs {1}{G} and also Foresees 1 at each Dawn.
- Granary of Rising Years costs {2}{G} and gains 3 life at each Dawn.
- Flood Before Noon also draws 2 cards.
- Two Harvests costs {2}{G}.
- Deeper Flood Channel also gains 2 life.
- Maret, Keeper of the High Flood lets you play 2 extra lands when she
  arrives, then draws a card.

**The Forge uses the new math.** At
[bladedarlings.com/forge/](https://bladedarlings.com/forge/), each keyword
shows what it is worth on your card and how that changes with Attack, and an
extra land drop says how many turns of extra mana it buys before the 10-land
cap. A spell that hits "up to two" targets now counts both, and a spell that
shrinks every creature is priced as the sweeper it is.

**Opponents read keywords the same way.** Medium and Hard opponents value a
keyword by the Attack of the creature carrying it. They know a flier that hits
for 5 is worth more than one that hits for 1, and they move a Hauntlink to the
creature that makes the most of it.

**Bosses.** The Drowned Deacon and the Marsh-Mother both relied on The Price,
so their decks were rebuilt to fight as hard as before. Sima Yi hits harder in
Warchest, and Hera in Darlings, because their decks now carry some of the
improved cards. Every boss was measured again over 200 games against each
starter deck and each Darlings precon.

**Replays** saved on 1.8.1 can't be played back on 1.8.5.
