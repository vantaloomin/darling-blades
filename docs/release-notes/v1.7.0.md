Starborne is here: 151 new cards, a ninth world, and the biggest balance pass
the game has had.

**A new expansion of living starships.** Starborne brings iridescent alien
crews, hulls that grow their own gardens, and the Mark: a +1/+1 counter that
spreads. Green grows Marks, red and white ride them into battle, blue moves
them where they matter, and black is the predator that eats them. Propagate,
the set's keyword, puts another Mark on every Marked creature you control, so
one early Mark becomes a board that compounds. All 151 cards carry finished
art, the set has its own icon (a seed of light), boosters, and six new tokens.

**The tower climbs to 24.** Chrome Broodmother waits at rung 23 with a swarm
that turns sideways all at once, and The Violet Signal Queen closes the climb
at 24, reading three turns ahead with an untouchable court. Both are measured
against win-rate floors like every rung before them, and the whole ladder was
re-tuned this release: four bosses that had drifted below their floors were
rebuilt on measured evidence, not guesswork.

**A new precon, and every weak deck lifted.** The Chrome-Violet Broodship
joins the shop: a green-blue-red swarm that marks its favorites and goes wide.
The audit that shaped it also found the weakest decks in the game and fixed
them. The Copy Kept in Linen, Flood Measures the Sky, Midnight Storybook, and
the Shadow Mandate starter all play meaningfully better than they did in
1.6.4.

**Cards read cleaner.** Every card now says "During your Dawn" for the start
of turn, "Mark" is a word the game teaches once instead of a sentence it
repeats, Propagate never explains itself twice, and card text follows one
consistent order everywhere: Skim first on its own line, keywords, effects,
Empower, and recursion last. The whole catalog was audited so it speaks the
game's own language.

**Lu Bu got what he always wanted.** The mightiest officer now costs
{1}{R}{R} and carries Rage: he attacks every turn if he is able to. Handle
with care.

**The numbers behind it all moved too.** Over three hundred cards across
every set were re-costed this release under a rebuilt power formula that
prices colour identity, pip weight, and drawbacks honestly. If a favorite
card feels different, it was measured against the whole pool before it moved.

**Quality of life from the debt list.** Your save can travel as a PNG card
with art you choose, draft rounds can be browsed after the fact, Limited
Warchest basics follow your deck's actual colour needs, and the release page
you are reading is generated by the pipeline that gates it.
