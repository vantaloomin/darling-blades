# Patch 1.9.1

A patch for 1.9. Art on the website loads properly again. 57 older cards
changed: some had near-copies at a higher rarity, and some gave their set a
keyword it was missing. Medium opponents got smarter about Ember-Flick,
Foresee and Duties. Hooves and Fire now comes with its tyrants. And nearly
every screen got a polish pass.

## In this patch

- **Art on the website:** card art loads reliably again
- **Hooves and Fire** now includes two SSR tyrants and Oru, the Tyrant Queen
- **41 older cards** stop being weaker copies of better cards
- **Every set has every keyword:** 17 cards gain one
- **Smarter Medium opponents**
- **A polish pass** on the menus, Shop, Collection, Deck Builder and duel

---

## Art on the website

In 1.9.0, some card art on bladedarlings.com came through broken or not at
all, because the site compressed the art files on the way out. A hotfix went
out on release day. 1.9.1 carries the proper fix: the art files now ship in a
format the site leaves alone. The desktop app was never affected.

## Hooves and Fire gets its tyrants

A player pointed out that Hooves and Fire was the only theme deck in the Shop
with nothing above Rare. It now includes **Ember-Crest Tyrant** and
**Fern-Crown Tyrant** (both SSR) and **Oru, the Tyrant Queen** (UR), still for
500 gold. They replace Rage-Kin Brawler, Blaze-Crest Tyrant and one Horn-Crest
Charger.

## No more weaker copies

Some commons did the same job as a rare or a card from another set, only
worse. Each one now has something of its own: a keyword, a different shape of
body, a stronger effect or a cheaper cost. Cards in boss and precon decks
changed with them, and those decks were checked against their win-rate
floors.

## Every set has every keyword

Each of the ten older sets now has at least one card with each of the
thirteen keywords. No new cards: 17 cards that fit their set gain a keyword.

- **Base:** Hecate, Crossroads Witch gains Dreaded.
- **Ragnarok:** Keeper of the Well of Urd gains Bulwark, Runecarver Adept
  gains Untouchable, and Hungry Shade gains Dreaded.
- **Celtic Fae:** Brigid's Ember Blessing grants Twin Blades instead of First
  Blade, and Laughing Pooka gains Rage.
- **Arthurian Court:** Mordred, Bastard Star gains Dreaded, and Errant
  Duelist gains Rage.
- **Gothic Monsters:** Lantern Patrol trades First Blade for Twin Blades and
  becomes a 1/3.
- **Dark Tales:** Bell-Tower Dancer trades First Blade for Twin Blades, and
  Red Hood Wolfslayer gains Rage.
- **Yokai Nights:** Oni Neon Marshal gains Rage.
- **Sands of the Duat:** Debt-Beetle Swarm gains Deathblade, and Prow-Fire
  Lioness gains Rage. (Ninth-Step Duelist's First Blade, above, closes the
  third gap.)
- **Starborne:** Lance of Two Suns gains Rage.
- **Drowned Deep:** Wrecker Mate gains Twin Blades, and Marsh-Widow gains
  Blood Oath.

## Smarter Medium opponents

- Medium now uses Ember-Flick and similar spells on its own Provoked creature
  at the end of your turn, when that wins it a card.
- Medium now values Foresee when it picks which spell to cast.
- Medium stops tapping a creature for a Duty after combat when it needs that
  creature to block on your turn.

## A polish pass on every screen

- **Main menu:** rebuilt with Play as the one big button and your active deck
  under it. The other screens sit in a tidy column beside Daily quests, and
  the version number is readable.
- **Gold** is written one way everywhere: a drawn coin on buttons, and "1,250
  gold" in text. The emoji icons are gone too, replaced by drawn icons that
  look the same on every device.
- **One colour language:** gold marks each screen's main action. Selected
  tabs and toggles get their own look, and text tabs are underlined.
- **Shop:** free deck claims stand out from the Buy buttons, and the strip
  arrows no longer cover the cards beside them.
- **Collection:** the filters line up on one grid, Owned only is a checkbox,
  and an empty search shows one clean panel.
- **Deck Builder:** count chips no longer cover card names, and the "+N"
  playset chip looks like a button.
- **In a duel:** the buttons never overlap at any text size, long opponent
  names wrap, and the tutorial's end screen matches the others.
- **Gauntlet, Limited, Settings and Achievements:** panels sit on the same
  margins as every other screen and fit their content. Limited's list buttons
  are bigger, so neighbouring + and - no longer overlap.
- Pop-ups dim the screen behind them properly, and disabled buttons keep
  their prices readable.
- High contrast keeps the background art on every screen.
