# Patch 2.0.1

A small patch for 2.0. The main menu and Settings line up properly on desktop, the duel portraits and Play tiles look the way they should, the Shop's pack row stops jumping, Story Mode's deck list scrolls on phones, and the opponent no longer acts while the Mandate seal is still in the air.

## In this patch

- **Main menu:** the Discord button, gold and Settings gear sit on one line
- **Settings on desktop:** the list fills the width of the screen
- **Duel portraits:** your life and your opponent's sit at the same spot on each portrait
- **Play screen:** no more faint lines across the tile art, and the art stays inside the rounded borders
- **Shop:** choosing a pack keeps it where it is, and arrows show the row scrolls
- **Story Mode on phones:** the deck list scrolls with a swipe
- **The Mandate:** the opponent waits for the seal to land before its next play
- **Card text:** Bounty of Demeter, Seeds of the South and Ariadne, Mistress of the Labyrinth read more simply

---

## Desktop menus

On the main menu, the Discord button, the gold count and the Settings gear now share one height and one centre line. On desktop, the Settings list now runs the full width of the screen instead of stopping partway across.

## Duel portraits

The life boxes on the two portraits sat at slightly different spots, so one looked off-centre. Both now sit the same distance in from their portrait's corner.

## Play screen

The dark fade behind the text on the Play tiles was drawn in strips that overlapped, which showed as thin lines across the art. It is one smooth fade now. The art on those tiles, and on your profile tile, also stays inside the tile's rounded corners instead of poking out past them.

## Shop

Choosing a pack used to slide it all the way to the left of the row, even when it was already in view. The pack now stays where you tapped it, and the row only moves as far as it needs to show the whole pack. On desktop, arrows under the row show when there are more packs to either side, and page through them.

## Story Mode deck list on phones

In Story Mode, the deck list for your heroine, Guan Yu's included, would not scroll with a swipe on a phone. It does now.

## The Mandate

When a player claims the Mandate, its seal flies across the board. The opponent used to make its next play while the seal was still in flight, which was hard to follow. It now waits for the seal to land.

## Card text

Bounty of Demeter, Seeds of the South and Ariadne, Mistress of the Labyrinth fetch a land from your Warchest. Their text also described what happens with no Warchest, which can't come up in 2.0 because every deck has one. That part is gone, so the cards now read "Put the first land of your Warchest onto the battlefield tapped." The cards work exactly as before.
